# Interactable buildings (decided)

Rules (decided):
- Three levels of building (option A). Most buildings stay solid blocks.
  - **Full interior**: walk in, several rooms, hiding spots. Only a few.
  - **Front room**: the door opens into one small room with the task in it.
  - **Shopfront**: used from the street (window, counter, door, yard). Nobody goes inside.
- Light (option C): some interiors are lit, some dark, depending on the building.
  Draft rule of thumb: **open at night = lit** (pubs, coffee house, surgery, watch house, newspaper night shift);
  **shut for the night = dark** (shops, workshops, offices, stores). Dark interiors need a lantern and
  count as dark for the Cutthroat's Shadow.
- Spots come from the role tasks (design/tasks.md) and common tasks. Exact buildings are picked in Studio.
